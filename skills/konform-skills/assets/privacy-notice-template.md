## 6. Your rights
<!-- DPA s.23(2)(e), (f), (g); rights in ss.37-41. The right to object must be brought explicitly to your attention (s.40(4)). -->

Under the Data Protection Act 2017 you can:

- **access** your personal data and receive a copy, free of charge (s.37). Send a written request; we answer within one month, which may be extended by one further month;
- ask us to **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data (s.39);
- ask us to **erase** your data, or to **restrict** its processing, in the cases set out in s.39;
- **object** at any time to processing of your data (s.40). **You can object to direct marketing at any time, and we will then stop using your data for that purpose** - use the unsubscribe link in each message or contact us;
- **withdraw your consent** at any time where we rely on it, without affecting processing already carried out (s.23(2)(e), s.24(2)) - for cookies use the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page;
- not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, that produces legal effects or similarly significant effects for you (s.38). [[Either: "We do not make such decisions." Or: describe the logic involved and its significance and consequences (s.23(2)(g)).]]

To exercise a right, write to [[privacy@example.mu]]. We may ask you to confirm your identity.

**Complaints.** You may lodge a complaint with the **Data Protection Commissioner**, Data Protection Office, Republic of Mauritius (https://dataprotection.govmu.org) (s.23(2)(i)). We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

## 7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies. Non-essential cookies (analytics, advertising, embedded media) are only used if you accept them. See our [[Cookie policy (link)]] for the list of cookies, their purposes and durations, and how to change your choice at any time.

## 8. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including encryption in transit (HTTPS) [[, encryption at rest, access controls, backups and regular testing]] (DPA s.31). If a personal data breach occurs we notify the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours where feasible and inform you without undue delay where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to you (ss.25-26).
